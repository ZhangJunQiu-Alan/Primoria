# Multi-Agent And Classroom Vision

Status: unapproved future product direction, July 2026. Nothing in this document
describes a current public capability or authorizes implementation.

## Product hypothesis

Once the personal adaptive loop produces dependable evidence, Primoria could
extend it into group learning:

- specialized Agents self-nominate or are delegated bounded tasks;
- teachers see aggregated weak-concept signals without exposing private learner
  detail;
- assignments vary by learner evidence rather than one fixed class sequence;
- group interaction produces reviewable signals for individual learning state;
- user-created Agents may be published only after ownership, safety, evaluation,
  and lifecycle contracts exist.

## Possible Agent model

An ecosystem Agent would need three independent properties:

- capability: what it is allowed and equipped to do;
- confidence boundary: when it should act or stay silent;
- track record: measured outcomes in comparable contexts.

Potential orchestration modes are explicit delegation, bounded composition, and
self-nomination. User adoption, rejection, follow-up, and correction could be
signals, but none should update reputation without abuse resistance and clear
causal attribution.

## Classroom dependency gates

Do not reopen classroom or marketplace implementation until all of the following
are true:

1. personal mastery and remediation decisions are dependable and explainable;
2. learner-memory provenance and correction are visible;
3. teacher/student privacy and aggregation boundaries are specified;
4. Agent ownership, versioning, moderation, deletion, and appeal are defined;
5. offline evaluation proves that multi-Agent routing improves outcomes;
6. deployment and cost limits support the additional concurrency.

## Explicit non-capabilities

The current product does not expose group workspaces, teacher dashboards,
user-created Agents, Agent bidding, public leaderboards, Agent marketplace, or
cross-user teaching optimization. Internal Tutor subagents are implementation
details, not marketplace Agents.
